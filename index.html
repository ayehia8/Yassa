Happy Anniversary My Love!!!

Two years! Two years!! Every time I think about how long we’ve been together, it amazes me. Somehow, it feels like I’ve known you my entire life, while at the same time I can’t believe it’s already been two years. I still remember our first days together as if I just experienced them yesterday. And I can’t wait for our two years to become four, then eight, then eighty, until the end of time.

Every time I write you a letter, words fail to express what I truly feel. No words are strong enough to describe my feelings toward you. Because after everything we’ve been through together over these past two years, no sentence I could ever write will truly be enough to express how deeply, completely, and endlessly I love you.

From the moment you entered my life, you quietly and gently rearranged everything. You made everything feel right. You changed how my days feel, the way songs sound, the way I see life, and even the way I see myself. You made my life brighter and lighter, and you became my greatest purpose and joy in life.

Being with you gives me a comfort I had never felt before, you feel like home. You are the happiness I never thought I would reach. Every little thing you do makes my life better. You have become, and always will be, my safest place and my one true home.

I do not love you only because you are perfect. I love you because you are perfectly, beautifully, wonderfully you. And to me, there is absolutely nothing more extraordinary in this entire universe than you.

Thank you for the best two years of my life. Thank you for your patience, for choosing me again and again no matter what, and for loving me the way you do. You deserve the absolute best, and I will spend every breath trying to give you that. The most important thing these past two years have taught me is that no matter where life takes us, I want to spend every single chapter of my life by your side.

Happy Anniversary, my love.

I love you more than anything, and I always will.
Forever yours,
Yassa
