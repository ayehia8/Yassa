Forever & Always

These are the vows my heart makes to yours — not just today, but every single day that follows.

♥
I

I vow to always choose you

Not just on the easy days, when loving you feels like breathing. But on the hard days too — the days when the world is heavy and everything feels uncertain. I will choose you in every season, in every storm, in every quiet Tuesday afternoon. Because choosing you is the easiest and most important decision I will ever make.

II

I vow to protect your heart

Your heart is the most precious thing anyone has ever trusted me with. I will guard it fiercely. I will never take it for granted, never make you doubt that you are loved, and never let a day pass without reminding you that you are my everything.

III

I vow to grow with you

We are not the same people we were when this started. And that is beautiful. I promise to grow beside you, not apart from you. To learn, to change, to evolve — always hand in hand. The person I become tomorrow will love you even more than the person I am today.

IV

I vow to make you laugh

Life can be heavy. But I promise to always be the one who lightens it for you. I will be silly when you need it, strong when you need that too, and always, always the person who makes you smile — even when the world forgets to.

V

I vow to love every version of you

The happy you and the sad you. The confident you and the uncertain you. The dreaming you and the resting you. I do not love a version of you — I love all of you. Every mood, every moment, every chapter. You will never have to perform for my love.

∞

I vow to love you forever

Not the kind of forever that people say carelessly. I mean the kind that lives in every glance, every touch, every morning I wake up and choose to love you again. If there is an after this life, I will find you there too. You are my forever. My always. My only.

✦
You asked me once how long I will love you. I did not answer right away. Not because I did not know — but because "forever" felt too small a word.
